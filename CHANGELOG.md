## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-10

- Validators and normalisers for Czech identifiers: IČO, DIČ, rodné číslo, bank accounts, IBAN and PSČ.
- Typed results with normalised and formatted values, not only booleans.
- Bank-code list and domestic account ↔ IBAN conversion helpers.
- Zero runtime dependencies; rules cited from primary sources.
